The joke is on
the algorithm.

The live investigator uses Qloo evidence and your answers to choose up to five predictions. NVIDIA hosts the model that directs the investigation. Recorded rounds use a fixed six-guess sequence. We also remove each interest, one at a time, and see how the same shortlists move.

“Predictability” means confirmations among familiar guesses. “Haven’t tried it” is not a wrong answer. Ranking sensitivity is separate: a winner needs a clear lead in two domains and agreement across both halves of the shortlists.

These are small, model-selected lists. Neither measurement says how interesting you are, who you are, or what you will buy. Shared and partial findings are labeled; only strict evidence earns a dominant winner.

Your privacy: search terms are sent to Qloo as you type (after two characters), before you start a round. Live interests and cultural evidence are sent to Qloo and NVIDIA; live reactions go to this server and NVIDIA to select the next test. Explicitly liked predictions may become additional Qloo inputs. Temporary game state is stored on the server for 30 minutes from creation; provider caches expire after two hours. Expired records are unavailable to the app and are physically removed on the next API request. This temporary state can survive server restarts. Hosting request logs include request URLs, connection metadata and headers; search text in URLs can therefore appear in those logs. This private preview requires ChatGPT sign-in, which supplies basic account details to Sites; the game does not store those details in its round records. Hosting-managed backup and log retention has not yet been verified. Reload or clearing your identity answer does not immediately delete the old server round or provider copies. There is no immediate server-round deletion control. Qloo’s privacy policy and NVIDIA’s privacy policy govern their data handling separately. To limit abuse, this server stores daily salted hashes of the trusted Sites identity, or the edge connection address for anonymous visitors. Raw identities and addresses are not stored by the game. Request and cost counters last up to 15 minutes; the ten-new-round daily allowance lasts until UTC midnight. Visitor-supplied forwarding headers are ignored. Provider-wide budget counters are retained for one day. Recorded-round reactions remain in your tab. No game account database, analytics or advertising profiles. Images load from the URLs Qloo supplies. Exporting a card or session is your choice.

The optional Identity Reality Check stays in this tab and is not sent to Qloo, NVIDIA or our server. Cultural influence means model sensitivity; identity attachment is your self-report; identity transfer through partnerships remains untested. Identity sharing is separately opt-in. Research export requires consent and review: no automatic upload or persistent research store. Delete downloaded files on your device; separately shared copies require a retention/deletion agreement with their recipient.

Recorded rounds use real, saved Qloo responses for an example profile. They are always labeled. There are no public result links in this game.
